# Chicken Overlord: The Great Purge

Fabric mod for Minecraft 26.3 / Java 25. This build implements a playable event loop on vanilla chicken and Ravager models: craft an Ominous Egg, survive daily armed-chicken waves, collect salvage and blueprints, then defeat the Overlord on the final day. Event state, blueprints, ranks, and Dominion survive world restarts.

## Install and build

Use JDK 25. Run `./gradlew build`, then copy `build/libs/battle-chickens-1.0.0.jar` and Fabric API into `mods` on a Fabric 26.3 client or server. For development, run `./gradlew runClient`.

## Configure

On first start, edit `config/battle-chickens.properties`, then restart the game/server:

```properties
event_days=7
hostile_chicken_cap=40
boss_timeout_seconds=1200
boss_terrain_radius=4
rocket_block_radius=3
peckzooka_pvp_damage_multiplier=0.25
dominion_enabled=true
rpg_cap_per_player=3
reward_multiplier=1.0
difficulty_preset=standard
gear_chance_per_day=8
event_cooldown_seconds=1800
natural_military_chance_percent=30
```

`event_days` is 1-30 game days; `hostile_chicken_cap` is 1-200 nearby Purge chickens per player; `boss_timeout_seconds` is 60-86400 (default 1200 seconds, one full Minecraft day); `rocket_block_radius` is 0-8 for player rockets; `boss_terrain_radius` is 0-8 for boss blasts; the PvP multiplier is 0-1; `rpg_cap_per_player` is 0-20; and `reward_multiplier` is 0.1-5.0. `natural_military_chance_percent` is 0-100 and defaults to 30. During a Purge, the chance rises by 8 percentage points per day and 4 per threat level above I, capped at 95%. `difficulty_preset` is `casual`, `standard`, or `hardcore`: Casual reduces enemy health/damage, Hardcore raises both and disables daily Rations Crates. `gear_chance_per_day` is 0-50 percentage points of additional scavenger armor chance per event day. `event_cooldown_seconds` is 0-86400 seconds. Set it to 0 for repeated test runs. Existing config files retain their saved values; set `boss_timeout_seconds=1200` for the one-day fight timer. Invalid values stop startup with the offending setting named in the error. A server's config controls the event for all players. Boss terrain damage still respects `mobGriefing`; the default radius 4 is intentionally destructive, so set it to 0 to protect builds.

## Play

Craft an Ominous Egg in a crafting table:

```text
FRF
GEG
FBF
```

`F` is a feather, `R` rotten flesh, `G` gold ingot, `E` egg, and `B` bell. Upgrade shapelessly:

- Threat II: Ominous Egg + gold block + netherite scrap.
- Threat III: Ominous Egg + ominous bottle + nether star.
- Threat IV: Threat III egg + blaze rod + Golden Feather; unlocked after rank III.
- Threat V: Threat IV egg + nether star + Overlord Shard; unlocked after rank IV.

Use an egg from your hand to start a world-wide Purge. Each Minecraft day is 24,000 ticks (20 real-time minutes at normal tick speed). The Purge does not create fixed daily chicken waves: naturally spawned chickens near players have an escalating chance to become armed hostile units, subject to the per-player cap. Their roles progress from scavengers and militia to sergeants, grenadiers, snipers, RPG chickens, medics, and riot chickens. RPG chickens fire three rockets at eight-second intervals with a visible marker and click-beep telegraph; grenadiers carry a custom 3D bomb model on their backs and visibly throw a primed bomb into its marked blast area. Day 5+ adds Ravager tanks or Iron Golem walkers; Day 6+ adds Vex drones. Day 4+ Phantom gunships carry a named chicken Gunship Gunner with a Beak-47, which fires at players in sight. On the final day, naturally converted units and ten-second reinforcement parties mix RPG chickens with grenadiers, snipers, medics, and melee troops; RPGs are capped per player rather than making up most of the army. Final-day RPGs have unlimited ammo. Medic Hens heal nearby Purge units and Day 7 bosses, including the Ravager and shield golems. A guarded salvage outpost spawns from Day 4 until the final day; raid its chest for rare materials, then defeat its guard to scatter nearby Purge squads. First kills teach and record blueprints and unlock the matching recipe-book plans. Survivors receive a usable Rations Crate at each day boundary except on Threat IV/V or Hardcore.

On the final day, the 4x vanilla Ravager with its 2.5x crowned rider and a mounted Overlord Sniper spawns 80-100 blocks from the player, protected by four tougher shield-generator Golems, two or three Overlord RPG escorts, and two Medic Hens. The 2.5x Sky Overlord Phantom and its chicken bombardier/gunner also join the same fight. The two bosses target players, never each other; the Ravager's ground charge can accidentally clip the Phantom, making the fight end sooner. Each individual hit on the Phantom is capped at one-third of its maximum health, so no single attack can remove more than 33% of its max health. Its own attacks target players and cannot hurt the Ravager or Purge allies. The Phantom now bites at close range repeatedly for high contact damage, and its spin attack sometimes launches visibly primed vanilla TNT; the occasional mega-TNT has a large lethal blast if you do not escape its marked landing zone. Combat warnings appear briefly above the hotbar instead of filling chat. These Golems use a dedicated entity type and only acquire player targets, even when struck by Purge units. The Ravager relentlessly pursues and melees the nearest player at a slightly reduced speed, smashes non-block-entity obstructions along its path (when `mobGriefing` is enabled), and periodically charges through its path toward the player's position from five seconds earlier. It can also leap onto a clearly marked player position for a heavy landing attack. Its slow, five-second sonic-boom charge tracks its marked player and projects a long-range blast that does not collide with its Ravager host; the marked beam provides a chance to dodge. Day 7 attacks deal 75% less knockback to players. The four shield Golems stay tethered to the Ravager, periodically fire high-damage red pulses without knockback, and leave blue soulflame at the laser's impact point for 20 seconds; touching it deals armor-bypassing damage equal to 10% of maximum health once per second. They periodically recover to a clear position near the boss if trapped or separated. They also power the boss's shield, so destroy them to expose the Ravager. Medic Hens heal nearby chickens, shield golems, and bosses. Purge rockets, grenades, and Ravager charges do not damage other Purge allies, except for the Ravager's intended collateral damage to the Phantom. The Ravager periodically calls up to six named Assault Chickens; these fast melee troops pursue and tackle players, and the mounted sniper telegraphs high-damage shots from up to 96 blocks away. Lightning telegraphs a marked strike before firing at a reduced cadence. The entire boss fight forces thunderstorms in every weather-enabled dimension, then restores the previous weather when the fight ends. Later boss phases add reinforcements and a wide orbital blast. Boss terrain destruction does not drop the broken blocks, reducing item clutter during the fight. The default meltdown timer gives the fight one full Minecraft day (20 real-time minutes); defeat both Overlords before it expires. If any player dies during the boss fight, all living players retreat more than 256 blocks for ten seconds, or the timer expires, the fight fails: players receive Darkness for ten seconds and loaded Purge enemies/projectiles are removed. A loss preserves salvage and blueprints and leaves an egg cache and bounded crater when `mobGriefing` is enabled.
During the final fight, two top-screen boss bars track the Ravager's and Phantom's health.

Craft the Scrap Bench with four iron ingots, four planks, and a feather. It opens the vanilla crafting screen whether your hand is empty or holding an item. The plan's weapons and utilities have working simplified abilities: the Beak-47 uses seeds, Rooster Spear has extended melee reach and extra damage to airborne targets, Flame Fryer uses Gunpowder Pouches and can ignite blocks when `mobGriefing` is enabled, Sniper Beak uses arrows, Medic Syringes heal, and the Egg Cannon knocks back targets. Flak Cannon, Cluck Shield, Feather Cloak, Riot Helmet, Reactive Plating, Cluck Radar, and Signal Scrambler are usable. Riot Helmet blocks frontal arrows while its three-second guard is active; keeping Reactive Plating in your inventory reduces Purge explosive damage, and holding Cluck Shield in your offhand further reduces it and reflects one nearby grenade/rocket hit back at its shooter. The General's Plume is wearable and grants Strength while Purge chickens are nearby. EMP stuns nearby units for five seconds. The Scarecrow Turret is craftable, attacks Purge chickens within 12 blocks, carries 64 seed rounds, and reloads 16 rounds per wheat seed. Bunker Blocks withstand the first Peckzooka hit and crack; a second hit breaks them. The Peckzooka blueprint is learned from the first RPG chicken kill; the weapon can also rarely drop. Craft Dud Rockets from a Rocket Casing, gunpowder, and paper. Right-click with the Peckzooka to launch a visible firework rocket; it explodes on impact, consumes a Dud Rocket, has a four-second reload and 120-shot durability, and uses the configured PvP and block-damage settings.

The Overlord Core is a one-use choice: right-click with it to call an orbital strike, or sneak-right-click a block to place a permanent Defense Core. The placed core grants nearby players Resistance and attacks Purge chickens in range. The Overlord's Crown can be worn as a helmet and grants nearby allies Strength; place it on a wall when you want it displayed instead. General's Plumes and Nemesis Trophies can also be placed in item frames. After reaching rank III, the victory spawns a persistent Veteran Rooster villager who trades War Feathers and Golden Feathers for Purge gear. Chickens alive near players when the Overlord is defeated are marked as survivors; while loaded near players, each has a small chance to lay a Golden Egg every ten in-game minutes. Golden Eggs hatch rideable Giga Chickens. A Warrior Chicken that kills a player becomes a persistent, hostile Nemesis; each later kill promotes it, improving its health and damage. Killing it drops a named trophy.

Outside an event, naturally spawned chickens have a configurable 30% chance to be replaced server-side with the summonable `battle-chickens:warrior_chicken` mob. Warriors have their own entity type, visible name, distinct texture, iron sword, and extra health; they remain neutral until attacked, then pursue and retaliate against their attacker. Test with `/summon battle-chickens:warrior_chicken`. During a Purge, a separate escalating chance replaces natural chickens with role-specific summonable types: RPG, Grenadier, Sniper, Medic, Riot, Sergeant, Militia, or Scavenger Chickens. Each mod-created chicken role has its own entity ID and matching skin; unconverted vanilla chickens remain passive `minecraft:chicken`. No timed quota is spawned outside the explicitly configured final-day siege. `/purge contribution` reports the current event score; boss damage, unit kills, outpost clears, and shield generators contribute.

For a quick smoke test, set `event_days=1` and `event_cooldown_seconds=0`, restart, then use `/give @s battle-chickens:ominous_egg` and activate it. `/purge status` confirms the event; on a one-day event the boss appears immediately. For the natural-spawn test, raise `natural_military_chance_percent=100`, start a Purge, and wait near a natural chicken spawn area; spawned chickens should become named hostile units, up to the configured cap. In separate tests, give yourself a Scrap Bench with `/give @s battle-chickens:scrap_bench`, place it, and interact to confirm the crafting screen opens; place a Scarecrow Turret, feed it wheat seeds to reload, and interact empty-handed to inspect ammo; place a Nemesis Trophy or General's Plume on a wall to display it in an item frame.

Commands: `/purge status`, `/purge rank`, `/purge contribution`, and `/purge blueprints`; operators can use `/purge stop` to end the event and remove its units. Contribution points come from Purge kills, boss damage, and outpost guards. Victory grants the Overlord Core to everyone, scales shard rewards to contribution (with a floor), and awards the Crown to contributors. The shield-only entity can be tested with `/summon battle-chickens:shield_generator_golem`.

## Chicken models and assets

Every mod-created chicken role has its own server-registered entity type; naturally spawned vanilla chickens that do not convert remain `minecraft:chicken`. Test individual entities and textures with commands such as `/summon battle-chickens:rpg_chicken`, `/summon battle-chickens:grenadier_chicken`, `/summon battle-chickens:gunship_gunner`, and `/summon battle-chickens:overlord_bombardier`. The complete IDs are `warrior_chicken`, `rpg_chicken`, `grenadier_chicken`, `sniper_chicken`, `medic_chicken`, `riot_chicken`, `sergeant_chicken`, `militia_chicken`, `scavenger_chicken`, `gunship_gunner`, `overlord_rooster`, `overlord_rpg_chicken`, `summoner_general`, `tank_general`, `bomber_general`, `buffer_general`, `overlord_bombardier`, `summoned_chicken`, `outpost_guard`, and `giga_chicken` (prefix each with `battle-chickens:`). Purged units still use vanilla chicken geometry, with role-specific textures, names, held gear, and behavior. The client renderer selects textures by entity type; custom geometry is not yet implemented. The best artist-ready approach is to reuse one chicken rig for infantry variants and reserve unique geometry for the boss, Ravager armor, and structures. Provide editable Blockbench `.bbmodel` sources plus exported geometry/texture PNGs. Entity art belongs under `src/main/resources/assets/battle-chickens/`, while renderer/model code belongs under `src/main/java/com/cluck/client/`.

Item and block texture paths are `src/main/resources/assets/battle-chickens/textures/item/` and `.../textures/block/`; the corresponding item/block JSON models are under `assets/battle-chickens/models/`. If starting with only three pieces, model the Overlord Rooster, the armored Ravager, and the RPG Chicken first. Keep each `.bbmodel` alongside its exported PNG so the source remains editable. Day 7 uses the large Sky Overlord Phantom model alongside the Ravager; regular Phantom gunships also remain.

I added provisional, original 64x32 pixel-art role skins for the custom chicken entities in `src/main/resources/assets/battle-chickens/textures/entity/chicken/` (`warrior`, `militia`, `scavenger`, `rpg`, `overlord_rpg`, `grenadier`, `sniper`, `medic`, `riot`, `sergeant`, `summoner`, `tank`, `bomber`, `buffer`, `overlord`, `bombardier`, `gunner`, `guard`, `summoned`, and `giga`). The client renderer selects each skin from the registered entity type. Item and block art is in `src/main/resources/assets/battle-chickens/textures/item/` and `.../textures/block/`. These are starter pixel-art textures and can be replaced directly, preserving filenames and 64x32 dimensions. The grenade's editable JSON cuboid model is `assets/battle-chickens/models/item/purge_grenade.json`; its held/backpack/projectile art all use the same texture. Chicken geometry remains vanilla; custom role models need a custom renderer/model integration.

## Scope

The design document is broader than this prototype. Implemented systems include the event loop, configurable threat/difficulty, naturally spawned escalating military chickens, mounted units, Vex drones and phantom gunships, medic/riot/support roles, salvage outposts with radar/jammer/shield coops, first-kill blueprint recipe unlocks, salvage, battle-tech items, a Veteran Rooster trader, contribution-scaled group rewards, increasingly strong Nemeses/trophies, survivor Golden Egg drops, the Giga Chicken, a seed-ammo Scarecrow Turret (64 shots; reload with wheat seeds), a placeable Defense Core, trophy item-frame displays, the scaled ground Overlord with mounted rider and sniper, shield-generator golems, a charged sonic boom, Ravager leap/charge attacks, red pulse/soulflame support attacks, heavy rockets and custom modeled grenades, reinforcements, orbital attacks, configurable bounded terrain destruction, a one-day meltdown/egg-cache loss state, Flockwar Horn reward, advancement XP rewards, and ten manually awarded advancements. Titles are shown by `/purge rank` and on victory messages; new rank levels award named vanilla-color banners. Coops are deliberately simple blocks: Radar boosts nearby Purge units, Jammer blocks Cluck Radar in its radius, Shield grants nearby units Resistance, and EMP destroys coops. Rank bonuses currently grant War Feather drops at I, extra Dud Rockets at II, the trader/Threat IV unlock at III, Golden Feathers at IV, and an Overlord Shard/title at V.

Remaining gaps versus the plan: no custom damage types, RPG pity timer, kill-feed, stage-timed Day 7 invasion, respawning outpost chest, full recipe tier-gating, cheaper Gilded Egg crafting, rank-IV cursed effects, rank-V endless mode, title tags above player names, chicken pet skins, dedicated trophy pedestal blocks, or custom entity geometry. Some rank perks and passive armor/weapon effects are simplified. Cluck Radar reports nearby units in chat rather than drawing a HUD. The Defense Core has a modest shield/attack radius rather than a full dome, the trader uses a vanilla villager, and Giga Chicken steering is approximate. Full multiplayer/release balance testing remains outstanding.
